The problem

Your first paycheck is also your first real test.

Young adults make consequential choices about credit, retirement, investing and debt with almost no opportunity to practice. Financial education explains concepts. It rarely lets you feel the tradeoff before the decision is real.

The thesis

Don't gamify finance. Make it playable.

No coins, avatars or arbitrary scores. Engagement comes from experimentation, consequence and replay. The simulation never tells you there is one correct financial life. It shows what changed, and lets you try another path.

How the simulation works

One financial year. Decisions that compound.

01Earn

See what reaches you after payroll, taxes and baseline costs.

02Allocate

Choose which financial containers hold the money you can decide on.

03Experience

Life happens: an emergency, a trip, a market drop.

04Learn

See the consequence in context, not in a textbook.

05Experiment

Clone decisions in the Lab without changing your Universe.

06Adjust

Replay the same year with the same circumstances and new choices.

The core mechanic

Your Universe is what you did. The Lab is what you could have done.

Orders, balances and consequences live in your Universe. Lab experiments are alternate realities that never overwrite your actual year.
